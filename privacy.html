Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10th, 2026

1 Introduction

QEDmn ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

2 Information We Collect

When you register or use QEDmn, we collect:

  • Account information: full name, email address, phone number.
  • Payment information:
    • For BaridiMob transfers: transaction reference/confirmation details you provide to verify payment. We do not store your BaridiMob PIN or full account credentials.
    • For in-person cash payments: the amount paid and confirmation of receipt; no financial account details are collected in this case.
  • Usage data: which courses/videos you access, progress, and basic technical data (e.g., device/browser type) to keep the platform working properly.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Grant access to purchased courses and track your progress.
  • Confirm and process payments (including verifying BaridiMob transfers or in-person cash payments).
  • Communicate with you about your account, courses, or payment confirmations.
  • Improve our platform and course content.
  • Comply with legal obligations under Algerian law.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4 Minors' Data

QEDmn is used by university students (ST stream), some of whom may be under 18. If you are under 18:

  • We recommend a parent or legal guardian review this Privacy Policy with you.
  • Parents/guardians may contact us at any time to review, correct, or request deletion of their child's information.
  • We collect only the information necessary to provide the course (name, email, phone number, payment confirmation) and do not knowingly collect more than needed.

5 In-Person Payment Meetings

For cash payments, meetings take place exclusively on the ENP (École Nationale Polytechnique, Algiers) campus at a scheduled time. QEDmn facilitates the arrangement, but the meeting itself takes place outside our platform. If you are under 18, we recommend informing a parent or guardian of the meeting details in advance.

6 Data Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the platform (e.g., video hosting, email services), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, if required by Algerian law or a valid legal request.

We do not share your data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

7 Data Storage & Security

  • We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
  • Your data is stored for as long as your account is active, or as needed to comply with legal/tax obligations (e.g., payment records).

8 Your Rights

Under Algerian data protection law, you (or your parent/guardian, if you are under 18) have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements (e.g., payment records).
  • Object to certain uses of your data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at qedmnalgebra@gmail.com.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new "Last updated" date.

10 Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data? Contact us at: qedmnalgebra@gmail.com
